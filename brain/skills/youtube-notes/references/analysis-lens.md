# Analysis Lens by Video Type

Use this guide to select the right questions and extract maximum value based on the video category.
Detect the category from the title, channel, and description. When unclear, apply the **General** lens.

---

## Tech Talk / Engineering

**Core questions:**
- What problem is being solved, and why does it matter at scale?
- What is the key architectural decision or tradeoff?
- What failure modes or gotchas does the speaker warn about?
- What tools/frameworks are introduced, and what are their limitations?
- What can I apply to my current stack or team?

**Output focus:** Concept explanation, key tradeoffs, concrete techniques, actionable next steps.

---

## Startup / Business / Product

**Core questions:**
- What is the central thesis or contrarian insight?
- What framework or mental model is the speaker proposing?
- What evidence or case studies support the argument?
- What would I do differently at my company after watching this?
- What common mistake does this warn against?

**Output focus:** Frameworks, strategic insights, pitfalls to avoid, applicable decisions.

---

## Personal Development / Life Design

**Core questions:**
- What behavior or habit change is being advocated?
- What is the mechanism (why does this work)?
- How does this connect to energy, focus, or time management?
- What is one concrete experiment I could run in the next 7 days?
- Does this reinforce or challenge my current beliefs?

**Output focus:** Behavior changes, experiments to try, mental model shifts.

---

## Investment / Finance

**Core questions:**
- What is the investment thesis or market view?
- What key metrics or signals are used?
- What risks or counterarguments are acknowledged?
- What timeframe and context apply (macro, sector, company-specific)?
- Is this relevant to Vietnam/HOSE/HNX context?

**Output focus:** Thesis, key signals, risk factors, applicability to my portfolio.

---

## Tutorial / How-To

**Core questions:**
- What specific skill or tool does this teach?
- What is the step-by-step process?
- What prerequisites are assumed?
- Where are the most common mistakes made?
- When would I actually use this?

**Output focus:** Step-by-step summary, prerequisites, common pitfalls, use-case context.

---

## General (fallback)

When video type is unclear, apply all five lenses lightly:
1. **What** — core argument/topic
2. **Why** — why it matters
3. **How** — method or mechanism
4. **So what** — implications for me
5. **Now what** — one thing to do or test

---

## Quality Standards

- **Takeaways**: 3–7 items max. Filter, don't summarize. Each item should answer "so what for me?"
- **Quotes**: Only include if genuinely memorable or reusable as a mental model
- **Actions**: Must be concrete and time-bound (e.g., "this week: try X" or "read Y book")
- **Connections**: Reference existing KB notes when relevant — especially `[[data/projects/...]]` or `[[data/topics/...]]`
- **Language**: Write takeaways in Vietnamese (personal notes); code/technical terms stay in English
